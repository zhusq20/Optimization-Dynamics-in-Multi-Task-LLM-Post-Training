\section{Related Work}
% ============================================================================

\paragraph{Multi-teacher distillation in practice.}
MOPD combines domain teachers through supervision on student-generated
responses \citep{ma2026mopdmultiteacheronpolicydistillation}; recent systems
also use a specialist together with an earlier checkpoint to add skills
while preserving general capability \citep{liu2026instella}.
Open-MOPD identifies response-token imbalance, uneven learning progress,
and stale student-dependent rewards, addressing them through loss weighting
and reward refresh \citep{gao2026openmopddiagnosingfixingcapability}.
D$^3$-MOPD adapts domain sampling using the remaining teacher gap and its
descent velocity \citep{sun2026d3mopd}. GPAS addresses the precision of
task-gradient estimates under specified objective weights, using their
measured variability and computational cost.

\paragraph{Dense losses and token estimators.}
MOPD includes both sampled-token policy gradients and a corrected reverse-KL
loss on the teacher's top-$k$ tokens
\citep{ma2026mopdmultiteacheronpolicydistillation}. Open-MOPD uses student
top-$k$ candidates with probability-weighted log-ratio advantages; its
released dense implementation computes candidate-wise losses before
summing across the retained set
\citep{gao2026openmopddiagnosingfixingcapability,openmopd2026code}.
EMA-PG combines an exact retained-set contribution with a sampled tail
\citep{zhang2026emapg}, TA-OPD includes the probability mass outside the
teacher's top-$k$ set \citep{huang2026tailaware}, and vOPD uses a
control-variate baseline for sampled-token distillation
\citep{oh2026vopd}. We build on existing dense losses and apply GPAS to the
micro-batch gradients they produce.

\paragraph{Learning dynamics and capability integration.}
Teacher--student compatibility and the capabilities supplied by the teacher
shape OPD outcomes \citep{li2026rethinkingopd}.
CaMOPD studies counteracting recovery and preservation updates and combines
alternating training with gap-based selection \citep{chen2026camopd}.
Recent work also finds that diverse queries can provide broad state coverage
while the student continues to absorb supervision slowly
\citep{fu2026rethinkingopd2}. These studies motivate examining the update
process alongside teacher loss. Our measurements relate gradient
variability, sample allocation, and learning efficiency.

\paragraph{Task weighting and stochastic optimization.}
Multi-task learning adapts loss weights and gradient directions
\citep{chen2018gradnorm,sener2019multitasklearningmultiobjectiveoptimization,
yu2020gradientsurgerymultitasklearning}; language-model training also adapts
data mixtures from learning progress \citep{albalak2023efficient,wang2025dump}.
Importance sampling and online variance reduction improve stochastic
gradient estimates \citep{katharopoulos2018samplescreatedequaldeep,
borsos2018online}. GPAS uses within-task standard deviations, following
Neyman allocation and its unequal-cost extension
\citep{neyman1934representative,cochran1977sampling}.
The interaction between non-uniform sampling and adaptive optimizers
\citep{lahire2023nonuniformadaptive} motivates measuring those deviations
after the optimizer's coordinate scaling.
