\section{Related Work}
% ============================================================================

\paragraph{Capability integration in multi-teacher distillation.}
MOPD combines domain teachers through supervision on student-generated
responses \citep{ma2026mopdmultiteacheronpolicydistillation}.
Open-MOPD measures integration gaps against separately distilled students
and addresses response-token imbalance, uneven progress, and stale
student-dependent rewards
\citep{gao2026openmopddiagnosingfixingcapability}.
D$^3$-MOPD adapts domain sampling using the remaining teacher gap and its
descent velocity \citep{sun2026d3mopd}. CaMOPD studies counteracting recovery
and preservation gradients and combines alternating training with
gap-based selection \citep{chen2026camopd}.
Our analysis distinguishes the effect of the chosen mean update from
uncertainty in its estimate. Our proposed checkpoint interventions test
whether increased precision improves joint progress under fixed weights.

\paragraph{Dense losses and token estimators.}
MOPD includes both sampled-token policy gradients and a corrected reverse-KL
loss on the teacher's top-$k$ tokens
\citep{ma2026mopdmultiteacheronpolicydistillation}. Open-MOPD uses student
top-$k$ candidates with probability-weighted log-ratio advantages; its
released dense implementation computes candidate-wise losses before
summing across the retained set
\citep{gao2026openmopddiagnosingfixingcapability,openmopd2026code}.
EMA-PG combines an exact retained-set contribution with a sampled tail
\citep{zhang2026emapg}, TA-OPD includes the probability mass outside the
teacher's top-$k$ set \citep{huang2026tailaware}, and vOPD uses a
control-variate baseline for sampled-token distillation
\citep{oh2026vopd}. We build on existing dense losses and apply GPAS to the
micro-batch gradients they produce.

\paragraph{On-policy learning dynamics.}
Teacher--student compatibility and the capabilities supplied by the teacher
shape OPD outcomes \citep{li2026rethinkingopd}.
Rethinking OPD~II finds slow alignment even when training states are held
fixed in its studied settings \citep{fu2026rethinkingopd2}. This motivates
examining optimization separately from state coverage, but does not
identify gradient noise as the cause. We therefore distinguish
fixed-reference loss, fresh-policy loss, and downstream capability in the
evaluation protocol.

\paragraph{Task weighting and stochastic optimization.}
Multi-task learning adapts loss weights and gradient directions
\citep{chen2018gradnorm,sener2019multitasklearningmultiobjectiveoptimization,
yu2020gradientsurgerymultitasklearning}; language-model training also adapts
data mixtures from learning progress \citep{albalak2023efficient,wang2025dump}.
Directional sampling tests already distinguish descent reliability from
norm accuracy \citep{bollapragada2018adaptive}, and adaptive sampling has
been developed for stochastic multiobjective optimization
\citep{zhao2024adaptive}. Our local analysis applies these established
ideas to domain progress in MOPD. GPAS minimizes total update variance,
following Neyman allocation and its unequal-cost extension
\citep{neyman1934representative,cochran1977sampling}.
The interaction between non-uniform sampling and adaptive optimizers
\citep{lahire2023nonuniformadaptive} motivates measuring variance after
the optimizer's coordinate scaling. We show why minimizing this
trace variance need not maximize each domain's directional reliability.
