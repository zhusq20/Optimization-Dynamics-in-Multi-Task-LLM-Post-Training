\section{Discussion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

The paper first explains loss weighting, then examines sparse updates
inside MOPD, and finally compares vocabulary-level supervision choices. Its empirical
center is the progression from observing sparsity to measuring
teacher-conditioned support overlap and relating support differences
to teacher distribution differences. Teacher--student distance and
signed cross-task effects aid interpretation without replacing those
questions.

The PG/top-$k$ comparison is deliberately bounded. Similar sparsity
in both single-teacher and multi-teacher settings is a useful observation
within the tested regime; it does not require an additional mechanism
program. A stable difference can motivate further analysis, but a
smaller support alone does not establish better capability integration.
Likewise, teacher divergence may or may not predict support difference,
and overlap may contain either shared or opposing corrections.
