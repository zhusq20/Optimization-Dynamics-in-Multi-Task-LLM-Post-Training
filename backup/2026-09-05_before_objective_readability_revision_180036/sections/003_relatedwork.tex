\section{Related Work}

\paragraph{Capability integration and task weighting.}
MOPD consolidates routed domain teachers in policy space
\citep{ma2026mopdmultiteacheronpolicydistillation}.
Open-MOPD diagnoses token-share and reward-related imbalances
\citep{gao2026openmopddiagnosingfixingcapability}.
Our first study audits the reduction used by each implementation and
separates cross-domain weights from within-domain length weighting.
The algebra explains existing objectives; it is not introduced as a
new allocation algorithm. Prior work on multi-task loss weighting and
gradient conflict further motivates distinguishing weight balance from
joint capability gains \citep{chen2018gradnorm,yu2020gradientsurgerymultitasklearning}.

\paragraph{Sparse updates and teacher compatibility.}
\citet{mukherjee2025subnetworks} find sparse cumulative parameter changes
in RL and substantial support reuse across settings.
\citet{yu2026denseupdates} directly establish related OPD signatures,
including teacher-varied overlap and functional masked-training tests.
\citet{shen2026opdgeometry} examine update subspaces and report rank
robustness to response-position thinning. These observations are
starting points for our study, not discoveries claimed here.
Teacher compatibility also affects distillation success
\citep{li2026rethinkingopd,ma2026mopdmultiteacheronpolicydistillation}.
We first verify sparse updates in our setting, then study overlap
between teachers acting on the same MOPD student and its relationship
to their distribution differences.

\paragraph{Loss support and gradient estimation.}
MOPD provides sampled-token and corrected teacher-top-$k$ objectives
\citep{ma2026mopdmultiteacheronpolicydistillation}.
Variance-reduced sampled OPD \citep{oh2026vopd}, exact-head plus
sampled-tail estimation \citep{zhang2026emapg}, and tail-aware losses
\citep{huang2026tailaware} show why token count alone does not identify
the estimation or optimization problem. Our primary loss study compares parameter sparsity under practical PG
and top-$k$ objectives in single-teacher and multi-teacher OPD. These
established controls are reserved for interpreting a stable difference;
we do not propose a new estimator.
