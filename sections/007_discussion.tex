\section{Discussion}

The experiments cover one model family and one training seed. The local supervision comparison uses four prefix banks with high top-$k$ probability coverage, limiting what it establishes about broader vocabulary tails or other student states. The bootstrap intervals quantify evaluation-question uncertainty at fixed checkpoints, not variability across training runs. These limits apply to the proposed recipe as well as to the individual comparisons.

Local changes in a distillation loss and long-term capability transfer remain distinct measurements. Student-generated responses evolve during training, so a fixed-state diagnostic does not capture the full trajectory. The recipe in Section~\ref{sec:recipe} uses such diagnostics to guide decisions, with stability left to prospective tests of domain retention under matched budgets and repeated training runs.
