\section{Related Work}

MOPD combines specialized teachers through feedback on student-generated responses \citep{ma2026mopdmultiteacheronpolicydistillation}. Open-MOPD studies capability imbalance and introduces token-share balancing, gap-based weighting, and reward refresh \citep{gao2026openmopddiagnosingfixingcapability}. Its token-share correction maps directly to domain-token averaging under the same batch and target domain weights. Our first study separates this between-domain correction from within-domain response weighting and compares their capability outcomes.

\citet{mukherjee2025subnetworks} investigate small parameter subsets that support reinforcement-learning fine-tuning. \citet{yu2026denseupdates} find concentrated checkpoint changes under dense distillation supervision and overlap between the changes produced by different teachers in separately trained students. Our second question concerns teachers acting from the same joint student and optimizer state. Our third compares vocabulary supervision through both parameter concentration and capability. \citet{shen2026opdgeometry} study OPD parameter geometry and select response positions for learning; their position selection concerns a different supervision dimension from the vocabulary alternatives scored at each prefix here.
