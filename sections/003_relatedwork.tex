\section{Related Work}

MOPD combines domain-specialized teachers by scoring student-generated responses, using policy-gradient and distribution-based supervision \citep{ma2026mopdmultiteacheronpolicydistillation}. Open-MOPD studies capability imbalance in this setting and introduces interventions for task weighting and response behavior \citep{gao2026openmopddiagnosingfixingcapability}. Our normalization comparison connects the choice of averaging denominator to aligned capability trajectories and recorded token allocation.

Sparse parameter changes have also been studied in reinforcement learning and distillation. \citet{mukherjee2025subnetworks} investigate small parameter subsets that support RL fine-tuning. \citet{yu2026denseupdates} study sparse changes under dense supervision, and \citet{shen2026opdgeometry} examine OPD parameter geometry. Our second study asks how this sparsity appears in a joint student and whether teachers affect overlapping parameters, relating the available gradient comparisons to teacher-distribution distance. Our third study directly compares update concentration and capability under sampled-token and top-$k$ supervision, with full vocabulary as a local reference.
