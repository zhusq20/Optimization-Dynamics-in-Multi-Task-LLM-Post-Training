\section{Related Work}

\paragraph{Loss normalization.}
DAPO replaces response-level with token-level averaging \citep{yu2025dapo}; Dr.~GRPO analyzes response-length and reward-normalization bias \citep{liu2025understandingr1zero}. We distinguish domain weighting from within-domain response weighting and measure their effects on gradients, Adam updates, and task scores.

\paragraph{Multi-teacher distillation.}
MOPD integrates specialized teachers on student-generated responses \citep{ma2026mopdmultiteacheronpolicydistillation}. Open-MOPD introduces domain token balancing, performance-gap weighting, and reward refresh \citep{gao2026openmopddiagnosingfixingcapability}. Its token-share correction corresponds to DT for fixed batches and domain weights; our comparison further isolates response-length weighting.

\paragraph{Post-training parameter geometry.}
\citet{mukherjee2025subnetworks} study small parameter subsets supporting RL fine-tuning. \citet{yu2026denseupdates} report concentrated changes under dense distillation and overlap between separately trained students. We compare teachers at the same jointly trained student and Adam state, separating FP32 from BF16 changes. \citet{shen2026opdgeometry} study OPD geometry and response-position selection; our supervision comparison varies vocabulary candidates at each position.
