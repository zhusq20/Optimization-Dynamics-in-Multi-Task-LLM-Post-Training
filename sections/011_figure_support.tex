\section{Supporting Views for the Three Main Figures}
\label{app:nine_panel_support}

The normalization figure uses six panels; the other two main figures use three panels each.
The following views retain the aggregate normalization trajectories, detailed optimizer intervention, update-250 joint geometry, and original M-PG/100 concentration measurements referenced in the text.
The normalization capability curves retain the original estimated seed values, as identified in their captions. The supervision capability contrasts use measured seed-42 scores.
The separate author-audit DR/500 calculation (two measured seeds and one retained estimate) is stored with its provenance and is not interpreted as three-run empirical uncertainty.
