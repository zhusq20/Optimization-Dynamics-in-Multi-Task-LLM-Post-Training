\section{Supporting Views for the Main Figures}
\label{app:nine_panel_support}

\paragraph{Capability provenance.}
Figures~\ref{fig:normalization_capability}(c--f) and~\ref{fig:normalization_aggregate_support} retain the original means and sample standard deviations from measured seed-42 and estimated seed-43/44 scores. The authors report agreement with subsequent three-seed experiments; the retained estimates are not independently verified measurements. The separate author-audit DR/500 calculation uses two measured seeds and one retained estimate and is not interpreted as three-run empirical uncertainty. Figure~\ref{fig:supervision_overview}(c) uses measured seed-42 scores; its contrasts are point estimates rather than tests of training-seed significance.

\paragraph{Teacher-update controls.}
Figure~\ref{fig:subnetwork_overview}(a) compares DR cumulative changes at update 500. Panels (b,c) use the common M-PG/500 student and optimizer state. Each bank contains six teacher pairs; large points average four banks. SGD and reset-$m$ steps are individually norm-matched to saved-state Adam. Teacher-pair observations share teachers and banks and are dependent. Steps use non-fused PyTorch updates on saved-state copies, rather than bitwise replay of the online optimizer. Single-teacher and joint runs differ in per-domain response exposure as specified in Appendix~\ref{app:setup}.

\paragraph{Local supervision controls.}
Figure~\ref{fig:supervision_overview}(a,b) uses four banks per joint-student state. Losses share prefixes and saved optimizer state within each row, while inputs may differ across rows. Direct T64 step-direction pairs were not recorded. Panel (c) matches task, setting, and update, including the completed joint update-500 evaluation. Table~\ref{tab:local_concentration} supplies the FP32 and energy-concentration measurements for the M-PG/100 comparison described in Appendix~\ref{app:losses}.

\subsection{Additional control figures}
\label{app:added_controls}

Figure~\ref{fig:normalization_interventions} reuses the six fixed banks and online clipping records described in Appendix~\ref{app:fixed_batch_normalization}. Reset-$m$ and reset-$(m,v)$ retain the optimizer clock. Panel (c) averages four domain probe losses within each bank; its error bars are sample standard deviations across the three banks at each checkpoint, not training-seed uncertainty.

Figure~\ref{fig:sampling_history_controls}(a) uses three M-PG/100 rollout banks with a 4,096-token response cap, containing 48 responses and 288 prefixes. Twelve fixed-prefix subbanks each receive two independent PG action draws at counts 1, 16, and 64; the I64 reference is evaluated once per subbank. Mean PG gradient cosines to full vocabulary are 0.5374, 0.8652, and 0.9667; I64 is 0.999988. These are local diagnostic repeats. Panels (b,c) instead use the two 256-token-cap banks from Appendix~\ref{app:records}; the zero-gradient branch is measured once. Reset-$m$ retains the second moment and clock, whereas fresh Adam resets both moments and the clock. These separate experiments do not form one paired intervention chain.

\paragraph{Cumulative trajectories.}

The single-teacher runs receive four times as many mathematics responses per update as the joint runs.
