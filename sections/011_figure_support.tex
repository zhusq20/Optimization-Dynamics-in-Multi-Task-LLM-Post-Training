\section{Supporting Views for the Three Main Figures}
\label{app:nine_panel_support}

The main figures combine the completed comparisons in three panels per study.
The following views retain the detailed optimizer intervention, update-250 joint geometry, and original M-PG/100 concentration measurements referenced in the text.
The new main capability contrasts use measured seed-42 scores; estimated seed values are excluded.
The separate author-audit DR/500 calculation (two measured seeds and one retained estimate) is stored with its provenance and is not interpreted as three-run empirical uncertainty.
