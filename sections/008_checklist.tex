\section*{Reproducibility Statement}

The accompanying evidence bundle contains the values used in every figure and table, source hashes, configuration records, a scoring audit, and scripts that regenerate the plots on CPU. Twelve capability suites are verified against response-level artifacts; four single-teacher suites use previously verified summaries from a second node. Appendix~\ref{app:setup} gives the evaluation protocol, checkpoint coverage, and uncertainty procedure. Appendix~\ref{app:records} specifies the parameter coordinates and local optimizer calculations.

\section*{Ethics Statement}

The experiments use public model families and benchmark datasets to study optimization and capability transfer. We report standard benchmark scores with the stated evaluation denominators. Generative AI assisted manuscript editing and analysis code; the authors are responsible for the reported measurements and their interpretation.
