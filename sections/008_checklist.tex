\subsection*{Ethics statement}

% (This section is \textbf{recommended} and does not count toward the page limit.)

The study evaluates optimization methods for existing language models with
established reasoning and instruction-following datasets and automated
verifiers. Model and dataset biases can affect the reported capability scores.
We record data provenance, invalid outputs, and verifier failures to support
careful interpretation of the results.

\subsection*{Reproducibility statement}

% (This section is \textbf{recommended} and does not count toward the page limit.)

Section~\ref{sec:experiments} specifies the model family, task setup, update
budget, evaluation points, and evaluation protocol. The appendix gives the complete
derivations. The supplementary experiment record includes frozen data
manifests, model and tokenizer revisions, random seeds, optimizer settings,
task-sampling logs, saved parameter and optimizer states, and per-prompt
evaluation outputs.
