\section{Setting and Objectives}
\label{sec:setting}

Let $p_\theta(v\mid h)$ be the student distribution and $q_d(v\mid h)$ the teacher for domain $d$, at prefix $h=(x,y_{<t})$. A fresh response $y$ is sampled from the student for each training prompt. Teachers score the same student prefix. We write $\sg[\cdot]$ for stop-gradient and suppress $h$ below.

\paragraph{Sampled supervision (PG).}
For sampled token $a$, the implemented loss is
\begin{equation}
\ell_{\mathrm{PG}}(a)=-\sg[\log q_d(a)-\log p_\theta(a)]\log p_\theta(a).
\label{eq:pg}
\end{equation}
The coefficient is detached, with no advantage or policy-ratio clipping. At a fixed prefix, fresh actions $a\sim p_\theta$ give an unbiased gradient of the full-vocabulary reverse KL,
\begin{equation}
\ell_{\mathrm{full}}=\sum_{v\in\cV}p_\theta(v)\log\frac{p_\theta(v)}{q_d(v)}.
\label{eq:full}
\end{equation}
This identity supplies the reference for the fixed-prefix sampling experiment in Section~\ref{sec:density}.

\paragraph{Intersection supervision (I64).}
Let $S$ be the rollout student's top-64 set, $T$ the teacher's top-64 set, and $I=S\cap T$. The online I64 loss is
\begin{equation}
\ell_{\mathrm{I64}}=-\sum_{v\in I}\sg\!\left[
\frac{p_\theta(v)}{\sum_{u\in S}p_\theta(u)}
\bigl(\log q_d(v)-\log p_\theta(v)\bigr)\right]\log p_\theta(v).
\label{eq:i64}
\end{equation}
Probabilities use full-vocabulary normalization. The student weights are normalized over the original $S$ and then masked to $I$; actor probabilities are refreshed at the saved candidate positions. An empty intersection contributes zero. The local diagnostic also evaluates a corrected teacher-top-64 loss (T64), defined in Appendix~\ref{app:losses}.

\paragraph{Experimental configuration.}
M-PG uses four routed teachers and domain response averaging. M-I64-DR, M-I64-DT, and M-I64-GT combine I64 with domain response, domain token, and global token averaging, respectively. S-PG and S-I64 use the mathematics teacher and mathematics prompts. Each optimizer update consumes 64 responses: 16 per domain in the multi-teacher runs and 64 mathematics responses in the single-teacher runs. Training uses seed 42 and a 4,096-token response cap. The capability results cover 22,360 responses across thirteen complete evaluations; full settings and checkpoint coverage appear in Appendix~\ref{app:setup}.
